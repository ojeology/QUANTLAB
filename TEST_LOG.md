# TEST LOG — Blind OOS validation (numbered, with hypothesis + reason + result)

Goal: a strategy that survives 2024/2025/2026 with good drawdown and profitable months, for a 2027-01-01 crypto bot go-live. All OOS walk-forward, fees 0.05%, crypto 1H unless noted.

## Phase 1 — Baselines from the frozen repo
- **T1. Raw Family A / Family C bot strategies.** Reason: what the repo already ships. Result: PF@cost ~1.0–1.1, weak; Family C even negative on a full year.
- **T2. R077 "holy grail" (breadth50 + VolCeil static).** Reason: documented best config. Result: PF@cost 0.75 → loses money after fees.
- **T3. Static filter sweep (q × VolCeil × breadth).** Reason: find a robust static filter. Result: NO config survives all three years (VolCeil helps 2024 but hurts 2026 — opposite regimes).
- **T4. Control 5m.** Reason: negative control (should fail). Result: correctly fails (too sparse).

## Phase 2 — SVM mean-reversion
- **T5. SVM q0.75 (original "champion").** Reason: ML filters the coiled signal. Result: PF@cost 1.25 (2026), 75% profitable-months. Good but q0.65 better.
- **T6. SVM q0.65 + cross-regime checks.** Reason: improve edge. Result: PF@cost 1.33–1.64; 2026 strong.
- **T7. 4H timeframe.** Reason: different environment. Result: only 56 raw trades/3.5yr — too sparse, edge is 1H-specific.
- **T8. 15m cache.** Reason: different environment. Result: 2026-only, sparse — unusable.
- **T9. Forex (Dukascopy).** Reason: different market. Result: BLOCKED (API unreachable).
- **T10. Adaptive VolCeil (regime-gated, |ema_dist_pct|>2.0).** Reason: resolve the 2024↔2026 opposition by applying VolCeil only when price stretched. Result: **SURVIVES ALL 3 YEARS** — 2024 1.18 / 2025 1.82 / 2026 1.53 (30-sym, fees).
- **T11. Champion deep-dive.** Reason: measure risk, not just PF. Result: MDD −9.4% @1% risk; 22/30 symbols profitable (broad); 15/27 profitable-months.
- **T12. Ensemble A∪B.** Reason: more signal definitions = more robust? Result: WORSE — 2026 PF 0.92 (loses), MDD −34%.

## Phase 3 — Drawdown sizing
- **T13. Universe/risk sizing.** Reason: 73-sym MDD was −26%, unacceptable. Result: **30-sym + 0.5% risk → MDD −3.7%, PF@cost 2.47** (both safer AND more profitable).

## Phase 5 — Small-account × implementability audit (T34, 2026-09-04)
*User question: the validated edge is a big-trader rate (~+100%/3y). Which risk-per-trade × R:R makes a $100 account grow without ruin? Answering forced an implementability audit of the headline numbers. All runs below: same 50-sym manifest (2023 fetched OKX + cache 2024→Jul-2026), walk-forward per-year holdouts, fees 0.05%/side.*

- **T34a. Exit-anchor audit of the T25/T27 trend leg.** Reason: reproduce T25d before trusting any leverage study on it. Result: the trend's `backtest_donchian` records each trade at the **exit** bar (`entry_time=df.index[i]` after close), so the RF-q65 "condition" gate trains on and selects trades by **exit-bar features** (post-entry info). Verbatim reproduction on the fetched data: PF@c **1.576 / 1.376 / 1.377**, $100→$198.79 — matches the branch log (1.570/1.393/1.392, $199.70) → machinery + data faithful. Same code, gate evaluated at the **true entry bar**: PF@c **1.106 / 0.982 / 0.929**, $100→$106 — **the live (entry-only) trend edge is ≈ zero** (raw trend is itself 1.10/0.94/0.90). TP variants TP1–TP3R are all negative mean R. → **T25/T27 trend headline numbers are not implementable as published.** The MR leg (`sim_symbol`) is entry-anchored and unaffected.
- **T34b. MR champion on the FULL 50-symbol 2023→2026 universe.** Reason: T13/T24 numbers used a 30-sym sample; check the broad universe. Result: n=297, PF@cost **0.739 / 2.151 / 1.610**, return@1% risk **+71.2%** (30-sym log said +71.3% — the *return* reproduces), but **2024 is a real losing year (PF 0.74)** and **max DD @1% risk = −28.2%, not −9.4%**. The 30-sym sample simply missed 2024's losing regime; 2025/26 carry the whole result. Robustness stats (profitable-months 15/30, 36/49 symbols profitable) still broad.
- **T34c. Small-account leverage × R:R sweep ($100 start).** Reason: the above. Results (compounding, chronological; MC = 4,000 paths): **MR RR2 (TP 2R / SL 1R) is the R:R optimum** — mean R +0.30, wr 47%, full-Kelly 14.5% (vs RR1.5 default +0.22 / 53% / 14.1%). **Recommended config: MR RR2 @ 1% risk/trade → $100→$204 (+104%, CAGR ≈32%)** over 2024→Jul-2026, MC P(profit) 99.9%, P(halve) ≈ 0%, realized max DD **−37%** — an ~18-month 2024→mid-2025 grind (trough ≈$63, mid-2025) followed by a +49% Aug-2025 month, then +28% in 2026. RR1.5 @1% alternative: $173, DD −27%. Everything ≥2% risk → realized DD −50%…−80% (small-account killers); risk >3% → MC ruin > 0. Trend leg + any TP and the 70/30 PORT sleeve lose on $100 (they inherit the un-implementable or ≈0 trend edge). Practical leverage: 1% risk ≈ ~1× notional (MR stop ≈1.15% of price) → 3–10× OKX leverage is only for contract fit, never the risk dial.
